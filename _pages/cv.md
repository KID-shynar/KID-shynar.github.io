## Research experience

### Cerebrovascular self-supervised pretraining
- Develop pretraining methods for TOF-MRA using a teacher–student EMA framework and masked latent prediction.
- Generate structural pretraining targets and contribute to model design, training, downstream segmentation validation, and experimental analysis.
- Contribute to project coordination and manuscript preparation. Ablation studies and result consolidation are ongoing; not yet published.

### Circle of Willis anatomical segmentation
- Contribute to anatomical label design, annotation, nnU-Net v2 baseline training, and error analysis.
- Work with a 16-class baseline trained on 221 TOF-MRA scans and harmonize anatomical labels across sources.
- Investigate small-vessel segmentation and class imbalance. Research and manuscript preparation are ongoing; not yet published.

### Additional projects
- **Pneumonia imaging and pneumococcal capsule research:** annotation-tool development, lung localization, pneumonia classification, and participation in gene validation experiments.
- **MRI superconducting joints:** experimental fabrication and evaluation work involving MgB₂.
- **Multimodal learning assistant:** project lead for personalized video-assisted learning and learning-process analysis.
- **Cervical cancer bioinformatics:** under a senior researcher's guidance, analyzed bulk RNA sequencing data from 276 TCGA cases, including differential expression, pathway enrichment, immune infiltration, and clinicopathological associations.
