<h2 id="research">Research</h2>

### Self-supervised learning for cerebrovascular imaging

**Ongoing research · TOF-MRA · 3D representation learning**

I work on self-supervised pretraining for time-of-flight magnetic resonance angiography (TOF-MRA), aiming to learn transferable representations of thin and branching vessels from unlabeled volumes. The project uses a teacher–student framework with exponential moving average updates and masked latent prediction, incorporating vessel masks, signed distance transforms, vesselness, and topology-related supervision.

My main responsibilities include model design, pretraining target generation, model training, downstream segmentation validation, and experimental analysis. I also contribute to project coordination and manuscript preparation. Current work focuses on ablation studies and consolidating results; the research has **not yet been published**.

This project received a Third Prize in the AI+ Special Track of PKU's 34th Challenge Cup. It also supported my First-Class Undergraduate Research Scholarship awarded in April 2026.

### Anatomical segmentation of the Circle of Willis

**Ongoing research · Anatomical labeling · Small-vessel segmentation**

I study anatomical segmentation of the Circle of Willis, where small vessel size, class imbalance, and structural discontinuities make learning difficult. My work includes anatomical label design, annotation coordination, baseline training, error analysis, and harmonizing labels across data sources.

We have trained a 16-class nnU-Net v2 3D full-resolution baseline on 221 TOF-MRA scans. I am now working on label harmonization and improvements to small-vessel segmentation. Experiments and manuscript preparation are ongoing; this work has **not yet been published**.
